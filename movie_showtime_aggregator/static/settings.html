Data integrations

Credentials are stored in the shared server volume and are never returned to the browser after saving. AMC enables official prices, A-List eligibility, and reserved-seat availability when permitted. OMDb enables posters plus IMDb, Rotten Tomatoes, and Metacritic ratings.

A-List pricing is shown as $0 only when AMC data matches the performance and official attributes make eligibility known. Missing AMC attributes remain unknown rather than being assumed eligible. Geographic plan-tier restrictions are not inferred, so this assumes your A-List plan covers the theaters in your search area.
